[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` 会从句中识别移除，所用的共享移除引导词列表与改写时相同（包含 "strip"、"nix"、"scrap"、"without the"）："strip shadows from cards" 会移除卡片的阴影，"nix the shadow and rounder corners on cards" 只移除卡片的阴影并让它的圆角更圆，而 "no shadows and borders on cards" 会同时移除两者，效果与 "without" 形式相同。
